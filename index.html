Privacy Policy

Legion's Commander (Android) · package com.ismail.legionscommander · last updated 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Legion's Commander ("the game") and the services it uses collect, and why. The developer is the individual developer who publishes the game on Google Play ("we").

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. The game does not have its own servers. Your progress, settings and language are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies / ironSource), which delivers ads from the networks ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Yandex Ads. To show ads and measure them, these services may collect: your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, language and country, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and used by these companies under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity / ironSource (LevelPlay, Unity Ads): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Yandex Ads: yandex.com/legal/confidential

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

All game data is stored on your device; uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it. For data held by the ad networks, use the links above or contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact
